Search my email for order confirmations for Pokémon TCG single cards from the
last 14 days (Cardmarket, eBay and UK card shops such as CardCargo, Japan2UK,
Titan Cards, Tyneside TCG, NMD Collectables and Radam's Poké Stop).

For each order, note the shop and the order number. Read ordered.txt and
ordered_arrived.txt in the current folder. If either has a
line containing " | <shop> | <order number> |", that order is already
recorded: skip it.

For every other order, add one line to the end of ordered.txt per single card
(one line per copy; skip sealed product, sleeves and other accessories):

<card id> | <order date as YYYY-MM-DD> | <shop> | <order number> | seller <seller> | <the card as the email names it>

The "seller <seller>" field is for marketplaces where each order comes from
one seller, such as Cardmarket ("Seller: KardKafe" in its emails, giving
"seller KardKafe") and eBay. Leave it out for orders from a shop itself.

The card id is the card's TCGdex id. Find it from TCGdex's set listing at
https://api.tcgdex.net/v2/<lang>/sets/<set id> (lang "en" for English cards,
"ja" for Japanese): use the "id" of the card whose localId matches the card's
number in that set, e.g. me01-161 or M2a-003. If the card is one of a
MEGA Dream ex (M2a) card's reverse holos, add " energy" for the Energy
pattern or " ball" for any other pattern (Poké Ball, Love Ball, Team Rocket,
...) after the id, e.g. "M2a-032 energy"; Cardmarket names these as
version V.1 (energy) and V.2 (ball) of the card in "MEGA Dream ex:
Additionals". If you can't pin down the card
confidently, write the card as the email names it in place of the id, with
its set and number, so it's still recorded.

If an email cancels or refunds an order already in ordered.txt, put "# cancelled "
at the start of that order's lines. A refund can be for only some of an
order's cards (a seller who turned out not to have one, say): then cancel just
those cards' lines, and add " (refunded <date of the refund email>)" to the
end of each. If the refund email names the cards, use those. If it only gives
an amount, as Cardmarket's do ("Amount: 3,70 GBP (4,50 EUR)"), compare it with
the card prices in the order's confirmation email: Cardmarket refunds in
euros, so the pounds can be off by a few percent (a 3,95 GBP card refunded
as 3,70 GBP). A refund near the order's total is the whole order. Cancel a
card only when one card, or one set of cards, clearly fits the amount; if
it's unclear which, change nothing and list the refund in your summary so
it can be marked by hand. Skip refunds whose lines are already cancelled.
Otherwise only add lines; never change or remove existing ones, and leave
ordered_arrived.txt alone.

Finish with a short summary: orders added, cards added, cards cancelled, any
card you couldn't identify, and any refund you couldn't match to its cards.
